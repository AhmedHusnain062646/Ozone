## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial boutique and calm atmospheric luxury aesthetic—evoking the sensory tactility of an archival fashion quarterly integrated with the discretion of an invitation-only private members' atelier. The interface is organized around the concept of pure, uncluttered atmosphere: boundless negative space, deliberate pauses between objects, hairline architectural precision, and uncompromising typographic authority.

The system explicitly rejects hyperactive SaaS conventions (such as pill-shaped neon CTA buttons, playful iconography, or aggressive drop shadows) in favor of restrained curation, museum-grade layout discipline, and quiet confidence. The visual narrative treats multi-vendor commerce not as a crowded bazaar, but as an ensemble of curated independent ateliers presenting limited editions to discerning collectors.

## Colors

The palette reproduces tactile paper stock, deep botanical inks, and precious metal accents.

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** The primary viewport rests on Bone Paper (`#F5F1E9`), establishing an organic, non-glare foundation. Interactive and elevated panels employ crisp Milk Surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed strictly with a 1px hairline border in Pale Linen (`#E3DDD0`). A graphite dark state (`#101415`) is reserved exclusively for back-of-house admin consoles and contextual nocturnal overlays.
- **Brand Primary:** Deep Ozone Teal (`#0E3B3A`) serves as the core anchor for dominant calls to action, brand marks, and selected primary navigation states.
- **Accents & Signals:** Refined Champagne Gold (`#C9A25D`) functions as an editorial accent for product valuation figures, verifications, and atelier badges. Signal Mint (`#7FE3C4`) provides high-legibility telemetry against dark graphite surfaces.
- **Typography & Neutrals:** Reading text uses saturated Ink (`#14181A`) for primary hierarchy and Muted Sage Grey (`#5E6663`) for metadata, sub-labels, and secondary specifications.
- **Functional Semantics:** Status indicators remain desaturated and architectural: Success (`#2F7D5B`), Warning (`#C58A2B`), Danger (`#B5483C`), and Info (`#3B6A8C`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural tension between high-contrast editorial drama, contemporary spatial clarity, and archival monospaced precision.

- **Display & Headlines:** Bodoni Moda provides dramatic editorial presence with sharp vertical stress and delicate hairlines. Headlines must be styled in sentence case or title case; avoid all-caps for long editorial titles.
- **Interface & Prose:** Plus Jakarta Sans governs standard narrative copy, form elements, descriptions, and functional interactions. Its geometric balance maintains legibility at dense viewport scale without competing with editorial serif titles.
- **Valuation, Technical & Tabular:** JetBrains Mono is strictly enforced for currency valuations, inventory counts, edition numbers, provenance hashes, SKU classifications, and metadata tags, ensuring optical alignment across tabular matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout operates on a balanced 12-column fixed-max grid (capped at 1440px for editorial showcases, expanding to fluid boundaries only within operational merchant consoles).

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays feature generous 40px (`2.5rem`) gutters and outer canvas margins of 64px (`4rem`), allowing layouts to simulate wide editorial margins in prestige print volumes. Handheld breakpoints collapse to 4 columns with 20px outer margins and 16px gutters.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical breaks separate product features, curated collections, and atelier introductions. Visual breathing room is considered an active functional asset rather than empty canvas.
- **Spatial Consistency:** Micro-spacing scales strictly within 4px base increments (`space-xs` through `space-xl`), ensuring structural rhythm from micro-tags to multi-column editorial lookbooks.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects diffuse, heavy drop shadows in favor of architectural hairline framing and tonal elevation.

- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is defined by crisp, 1px geometric borders (`#E3DDD0`) setting off pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) from the bone paper canvas (`#F5F1E9`).
- **Surface Layering:** Stacking hierarchy is communicated through subtle contrast shifts. The base canvas sits at `#F5F1E9`, elevated cards sit at `#FFFFFF`, and focused operational overlays utilize deep graphite (`#101415`) with hairline mint or bone contours.
- **Ephemeral State Shadow:** Floating modals and flyout shopping satchels use an imperceptible, ambient contact diffusion: `0 8px 32px rgba(20, 24, 26, 0.04)`, anchoring the surface without disrupting the printed editorial character of the interface.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly adheres to subtle, tailored contours. All interactive controls, cards, modals, and preview media use a disciplined corner radius of 4px (`roundedness: 1`), scaling to a maximum of 6px on expansive dialogue sheets. 

Fully rounded pill buttons (`border-radius: 9999px`) are categorically prohibited across the system. Sharp, precise corners reinforce the architectural aesthetic of fine paper goods, bespoke stationery, and crafted packaging.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background Deep Ozone Teal (`#0E3B3A`), text Milk (`#FFFFFF`), 4px corner radius, minimum height 48px to satisfy touch criteria. Hover transition shifts background subtly to `#164C4B` with zero elevation jump.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#E3DDD0`. Ink text (`#14181A`). On hover, background shifts to `#FFFFFF` with border darkened to `#14181A`.
- **Text Action:** Unbordered, styled with uppercase `label-caps`, accompanied by a trailing hairline directional arrow (`→`) offset by 8px.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Containers feature a 1px hairline border (`#E3DDD0`), bone paper or milk fill, and 4px radius.
- Minimum field height is 48px with 16px horizontal internal padding.
- Focused inputs replace the border with `#0E3B3A` and reveal micro-labels rendered in `label-caps` (`#5E6663`).

### Cards & Curated Product Tiles
- Built with a Milk (`#FFFFFF`) surface and 1px Pale Linen border (`#E3DDD0`).
- Imagery preserves uncropped editorial aspect ratios (typically 4:5 or 3:4).
- Prices are rendered exclusively in JetBrains Mono (`label-numeric`), accented with Champagne Gold (`#C9A25D`) for limited edition listings.
- Hover interactions avoid physical lift; instead, secondary angles crossfade smoothly over 400ms accompanied by an understated reveal of the atelier badge.

### Atelier & Provenance Badges
- Compact rectangular badges styled with `label-caps`, 4px radius, 1px hairline border.
- Verified atelier marks utilize Champagne Gold borders (`#C9A25D`) on an ivory wash (`rgba(201, 162, 93, 0.08)`) with matching gold typography.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- Square checkboxes (4px radius) and crisp circular radios framed with 1px `#5E6663`.
- Active states fill with Deep Ozone Teal (`#0E3B3A`) displaying pure white minimalist indicators. No playful bounce animations; transitions are precise linear fades (150ms).

### Lists & Atelier Directories
- Divided by 1px hairline horizontal rules (`#E3DDD0`).
- Row items maintain a minimum 56px height, displaying atelier title in Bodoni Moda (`headline-sm`), category metadata in Plus Jakarta Sans (`body-sm`), and inventory counters in JetBrains Mono (`label-numeric`).
